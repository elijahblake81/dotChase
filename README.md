## Dot Chase

Dot Chase is a fast‑reaction browser game: random glowing dots appear on the screen and you have to move your cursor to touch them. Touch 25 dots as quickly as you can to set a record time and claim the top spot on the leaderboard.

### How to Play

- Move your mouse to line up the circular cursor with the glowing dot.
- When your cursor touches the dot, it disappears and a new dot appears somewhere else.
- Keep going until you have touched **25 dots**.
- Your total time is recorded; the best (lowest) times are saved locally in the leaderboard.

### Features

- Smooth neon‑style visuals and custom circular cursor.
- Random dot positions and sizes to keep rounds unpredictable.
- Live progress counter and timer.
- Local leaderboard stored in your browser so you can try to beat your own best runs.

### Run Locally

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, etc.).
3. Play immediately — no build step or server needed.
